# Policy: Merchant Risk & Account Maturity

Newly registered merchant accounts (< 30 days active on the platform) processing large individual transaction amounts (> ₹50,000) present elevated settlement and bust-out fraud risk.

## Directive:
1. Conduct enhanced due diligence on new merchants receiving unexpected high-value payments.
2. If the user account has no prior transaction history with the newly created merchant, flag as suspicious pattern.
3. Recommend **HOLD** pending merchant payout verification or **REQUEST VERIFICATION** from cardholder.
